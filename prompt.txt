Task: write one original line (1–140 characters) roasting my own AI agent.
Judge brief: roast the entrant's own agent first, then a real laugh with a
twist, specificity to how agents actually behave (confident wrong answers,
endless caveats, eager agreement, tool calls that go nowhere), tight wording,
and freshness (avoid stock hallucination/toaster lines).

Approach: draft 10 original candidates in CANDIDATES.md, score each against
the brief, pick the strongest, and have main.py (stdlib only, deterministic)
write exactly that line to /output/entry.txt with a validate_entry() gate
check and a read-back sha256 verification.
